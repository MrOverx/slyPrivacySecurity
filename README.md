## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Slyxy** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information and files remain strictly confidential and secure. ## 2. Information We Access and How It Is Used
Slyxy operates primarily on-device. The data processed by each core feature is outlined below:
### A. File Explorer & Document Management
- **Data Accessed:** Local files, documents, images, audio, video, and archives stored on your device.
- **Purpose:** Enables you to browse, search, open, rename, copy, move, delete, compress, merge, and organize files stored across your phone's storage.
- **Handling:** All file operations are executed locally using on-device filesystem calls. None of your personal files or folder trees are ever transmitted to or stored on external servers.
### B. Secret Vault (Encrypted Storage)
- **Data Accessed:** Private files you explicitly choose to import into the vault.
- **Security & Cryptography:** Files imported into the Secret Vault are encrypted on-device using industry-standard **AES-256** encryption. Your master PIN is hashed locally using **SHA-256** and is never stored in plaintext or transmitted across the network.
- **Data Access:** Only you can access your vault contents by entering your master PIN. If you forget your PIN or clear app data, the encrypted contents cannot be decrypted by anyone, including the developer.
### C. Safe Web Browser
- **Data Accessed:** URLs entered by the user, search queries, and webpage cache.
- **Content Filtering:** Slyxy includes an integrated, non-bypassable adult and harmful content filter designed to protect students and prevent exposure to explicit or malicious web domains.
- **History & Tabs:** Your browsing tabs and temporary web sessions are maintained locally and can be cleared at any time. We do not maintain any user browsing logs or selling profiles.
- **Third-Party Search Engines:** When conducting searches, queries are sent to your chosen third-party search engine (e.g., DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing, Brave). Use of these search engines is governed by their respective privacy policies.
### D. Multi-Utility Toolbox
- **Tools Provided:** Image conversion, image compression, image cropping, PDF merging, PDF splitting, image-to-PDF compilation, document watermarking, video trimming/compression, audio conversion, and QR code tools.
- **Handling:** All media processing and document conversions are performed **100% locally on your device hardware**. Processed output files are saved directly to your device storage (e.g., public Downloads or selected destination).
