## 📑 Overview
Slyxy is committed to protecting your personal information through industry-leading encryption and **privacy-first practices**. Our architecture is built on the principle of **minimal data collection**.

---

## 🔒 Security Architecture

### 1. Minimal Data Footprint of only in app
We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. 
* **Session-Based Handling:** User profiles, messages, and preferences are processed exclusively within your active session.
* **Zero Retention:** Data is never retained on our servers beyond what is strictly necessary for real-time functionality.

### 2. End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)
Both our **frontend and backend infrastructure** employ robust encryption protocols:
* **In-Transit:** All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted to prevent unauthorized access.
* **At-Rest (Local):** Any information stored locally on your device—including chat history and customizations—is protected using **Advanced Encryption Standards (AES)**.

### 3. Total Anonymity
We have eliminated unnecessary tracking at every level of the application:
* 🚫 **No Location Tracking:** We do not monitor your physical coordinates.
* 🚫 **No Conversation Monitoring:** Your private chats remain private.
* 🚫 **No Ad Profiling:** We do not build marketing profiles based on your behavior.
